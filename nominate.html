About the Award

Your nomination should be based on the significant voluntary activities the nominee has undertaken within the NT community — including service organisations, charities, welfare work, acts of valor or humanitarianism, across all years of their life.

Principal Requirements

  • check_circle Nominee must be 65 years or older at time of nominations closing. Date of birth is mandatory.
  • check_circle Nomination must be based on voluntary work contributed to the NT community.
  • check_circle A seconder is mandatory. Addresses, emails and phone numbers required.
  • check_circle Attach any supporting documents. The more detail provided, the easier it is to assess validity.
